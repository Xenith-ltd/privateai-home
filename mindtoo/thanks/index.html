What happens next

1. Find your key

Polar emails your receipt, with your licence key, straight after you buy. It comes from Polar rather than from us, so if it has not arrived, look in your spam folder.

Your key is also on your Polar purchases page. Sign in there with the email you bought with.

2. Activate MindToo

Open MindToo, go to Settings, and paste the key under Licence. It is checked with the shop once, and then once a week.

3. Your other computers

The same key works on up to three of your own computers. If you replace one, free its place on your Polar purchases page, or write to us and we will.

Anything wrong?

Write to support@privateai.ltd. We read every message, and if something is wrong we will look at it with you.
