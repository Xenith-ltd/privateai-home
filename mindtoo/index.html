What it does

It remembers everything.

Every word, kept

Each message you send and each reply you get is kept as it happens, in Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor and Windsurf. In the Claude app, MindToo’s skill and plugin keep each turn too.

Handed over first

Before an assistant answers, MindToo gives it what it holds that bears on your message, from every assistant you use.

Honest about what it knows

When nothing it holds answers a question, it says so plainly.

Search by meaning

It finds what you meant even when none of your words match, using a small model that runs inside the app.

Corrected everywhere

Tell any assistant it has something wrong, and the correction replaces it for all of them. The old version stays in the history.

Standing instructions

Pin what matters, and every assistant sees it at the start of every conversation.

Your past, brought with you

Start with what you already have.

Bring in the conversations Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI already hold on your computer, and the data exports of the Claude app and ChatGPT.

See what ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini have remembered about you, and keep it one statement at a time.

Give an agent its own record: a message to an agent reaches only that agent, and a folder can belong to one.

Private by construction

Yours, on your computer.

Where it lives

In one file in your own Windows user folder. There is no MindToo account and no MindToo cloud.

What goes online

Your licence key, to the shop, when it is activated and re-checked. And a key in your API Wallet, to its own provider, when you press Test. Nothing else.

Only you delete

Assistants can remember, correct and archive. Deleting for good is yours alone.

Your controls

Pause it for every assistant, or lock it with a passcode. Give each assistant full, read-only or no access. Keep topics and conversations private from all of them.

Your keys

The API Wallet keeps your keys with Windows' own encryption. Assistants can see a key’s name, never its value.

Leave whenever you like

Export everything at any time, and make a backup whenever you want one. Reading, search and export never lock, even after the trial.

Questions

Asked before.

Does anything leave my computer?

Only your licence key, to the shop, and a wallet key to its own provider when you test it. Search by meaning runs inside the app.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Install it, open it, and connect the assistants it finds.

What happens when the trial ends?

Everything you have kept stays readable, searchable and exportable. Keeping new things waits until you add a licence. Your memory is never held hostage.

Can an assistant delete my memories?

No. An assistant can archive a memory, which hides it and keeps it recoverable. Only you can delete for good.

What if it gets something wrong?

Tell your assistant, and it corrects the memory for every assistant. The old version stays in the history.

Can I get a refund?

The 14-day free trial is there so you can decide before you pay: nothing is locked during it. There is no automatic refund. If something is wrong after you buy, write to support@privateai.ltd and we will consider it.
